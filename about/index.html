About us

People are the foundation of every organization

Parvathi Vrushabha HR Solution LLP is a Bengaluru-based Human Resource and Workforce Solutions company committed to providing reliable and customized HR services to businesses across various industries.

Talk to us Our services

Who we are

Reliable, customized HR & workforce services

We specialize in manpower services, staffing-placement, campus recruitment, facility management, payroll management, HR audits and statutory compliance support. We provide skilled, semi-skilled and unskilled manpower to meet the diverse workforce requirements of our clients.

With industry knowledge, professional expertise and a customer-focused approach, we deliver efficient and quality HR solutions. Our services go beyond recruitment, focusing on employee welfare, retention, training, workplace safety and effective workforce management.

Our goal is simple — to connect the right people with the right organizations and empower businesses through effective workforce solutions.
