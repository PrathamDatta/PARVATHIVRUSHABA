FAQ

Compliance questions

Which registrations does Parvathi Vrushabha maintain?

We maintain the applicable business registrations and statutory compliance documentation required for our operations, including LLP incorporation, PAN, GST, Shops & Commercial Establishments, EPF, ESI, Professional Tax and MSME / Udyam registrations, plus Contract Labour Licence and labour law registrations as applicable. Documentation is available on request, subject to applicability and current validity.

Can you help us with EPF, ESI and Professional Tax?

Yes. We provide EPF, ESI, Professional Tax and Labour Welfare Fund support as part of our Payroll Management & HR Compliance services.

Do you handle contract labour and factory compliance?

We coordinate contract labour and factory compliance requirements for clients, alongside gratuity, bonus and employee benefits administration.
